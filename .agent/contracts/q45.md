## Agent-side evidence (X1, `.agent/measurements/x1_transforms.py`, `expected/X1.json`)

Nine candidates: of one vocabulary series (raw, index, group sum/mean/min/max, count) — `square`, `cumsum`, `share` (v/Σ), `percent` (100·v/Σ), `diff` (consecutive); of two numeric columns per row and per group sum — `ratio`, `difference`, `product`, `total`.
