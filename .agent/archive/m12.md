  0.998** ⇒ at this scale the bitmask costs nothing measurable.

**Method note worth keeping.** The bug was invisible to the entire hardware-free suite and to
M12.3a's 20 green predicates, because every one of them asserts about the CALL — what
`compile_json_schema` receives, which processor is attached, when it is constructed — and none can
observe what the grammar does to a real decode. A live oracle was the only instrument that could
see it, which is exactly why the unit exists. The lesson generalizes: an argument-shape assertion
is a pin on plumbing, never evidence about behaviour.
